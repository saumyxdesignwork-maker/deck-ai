# Playful — Style DNA

White pages written entirely in one electric-blue pen. Lowercase bold monospace titles, with big ragged monospace paragraphs underneath and tiny mono notes tucked into corners and up the edges. Every title gets a hand-drawn mark (a loop, a squiggle, a double underline or a burst of rays), and curved marker arrows point the eye at what comes next. Photography is black and white and casual, in pairs, rows and collages. Titles wander: left, right, or turned on their side beside a thick marker stroke. Signature: a hand-drawn loop around the cover title.

## Signature moves
1. One ink colour (electric ultramarine) for all type and marks on white.
2. Lowercase bold monospace titles; big regular monospace body text.
3. A hand-drawn marker mark on every title, never the same one twice in a row.
4. Curved hand-drawn arrows linking titles to content.
5. Black-and-white photos in pairs, rows and collages; rotated vertical titles.

## Never
- A second colour (photos stay black and white)
- Capitalised or title-case headings
- Proportional (non-mono) typefaces
- Ruler-straight decorative lines (every mark is hand-drawn)
- Boxes, cards, fills or shadows

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** creative and youth-brand proposals, agency pitches, workshops, culture projects, student and community work
- **Not for:** finance, legal or medical decks; formal board reporting
