# Gradient Burn — Style DNA

Ember-black grounds that glow from the edges, broken by dusk washes and the occasional pale breath slide. Squarish, slightly condensed caps set centred and tight, with a clean geometric sans for reading. Tiny tracked caps labels float above every title. There are no rules or boxes; the only ornament is light itself: radial burns, hot horizons, edge flares and a ringed planet, all finished with fine film grain. Heat (ember, flame, pink) pools low and at the edges, while cool (violet, cobalt, lilac) hangs high. Text always sits in the calm centre of the field. Signature: every slide is a different burn of the same six colours.

## Signature moves
1. A full-bleed gradient field on every slide, chosen so the same field never appears twice in a row.
2. Field hues rotate through a fixed six-colour heat/cool ramp, so each slide burns a different combination.
3. Centred caps stacks in a squarish condensed face, with a small tracked label above.
4. Film grain over everything.
5. One gradient-filled giant word on a pale ground (cover or closing only).

## Never
- Boxes, borders, cards with strokes, drop shadows
- Hard-edged shapes or flat geometric ornaments
- A field peaking directly behind body text
- More than one pale slide in a row
- Lower-case display titles
- Gradient text anywhere except the single word-fill moment

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** keynotes, vision and strategy narratives, product launches, research reveals, AI and tech topics
- **Not for:** dense financial reporting, legal or compliance packs, anything printed in greyscale
