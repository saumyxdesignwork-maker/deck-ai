# Data First — Style DNA

A near-black page on every slide, quiet and precise. A light neo-grotesk set large in sentence case, with part of each heading dropped to grey so the white words carry the point. Labels are small, plain and numbered, never shouted in caps. The ornament system is the data drawing itself: hairline baselines, leader lines, semicircle arcs, dotted rings and dotted orbits. Greyscale does all the work, and exactly one signal colour marks the datum that matters (a filled gauge, an active node, a highlighted row). Diagrams take at least half the slide; text stays left and short. Signature: one coloured dot in a field of grey lines.

## Signature moves
1. A hairline or dotted diagram on every content slide: arcs, rings, orbits, leader lines.
2. Two-tone headings, where the muted lead-in is grey and the point is white.
3. A single signal colour, used only for emphasis data.
4. Light-weight display grotesk with tight tracking, in sentence case.
5. Bracketed page marker top-right, with a running label top-left.

## Never
- Caps headings or tracked caps labels
- More than one signal colour on a slide
- Filled cards with borders or drop shadows
- Gradients except the sphere and the wave lenses
- Photography
- Decorative shapes that do not encode data or structure

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** research readouts, market sizing, methodology, product and design process, investor updates
- **Not for:** emotional or brand storytelling, kids/education, anything that needs photography
