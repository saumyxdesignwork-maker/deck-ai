# Blossom — Style DNA

A white page holding one soft lavender panel, like a printed brand book. Headlines are light, airy Raleway in coral on the lavender panels and near-black on the pale ones. One italic serif word adds warmth to a big uppercase line. Reading text is small and generous, often in a narrow sidebar under a short hairline. Photos are soft and close-cropped and bleed off the edge of the page. The only loud note is a lime pill for the call to action. Signature: a coral wordmark floating high in a lavender panel.

## Signature moves
1. White page margin around a soft panel: lavender for covers, quotes, chapters and the sign-off; pale lilac with a hairline frame for content.
2. Light-weight Raleway headlines; coral on lavender, near-black elsewhere.
3. One italic serif accent word inside an uppercase headline.
4. A narrow sidebar column with a short rule, set against a bleeding photo or a hairline divider.
5. A lime pill for the one action that matters; tiny running header and folio.

## Never
- Heavy or condensed headline weights
- Saturated full-bleed grounds or dark slides
- More than one accent colour on a slide besides coral (lime is for the pill only)
- Drop shadows heavier than a soft card lift
- Busy ornament: no stars, scribbles or patterns

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** brand guidelines, wellness, beauty and lifestyle brands, marketing playbooks, calm product launches
- **Not for:** dense data reporting, engineering reviews, urgent or high-energy pitches
