# Sunflower — Style DNA

Warm sand-coloured paper and a small, quiet grotesk, interrupted by near-black chapter pages where a golden halftone sun breaks in from the right edge. Titles stay small and sit in the top-left corner like labels in a brand book; reading text is tiny and set in a narrow column on the right. One big paragraph per deck carries the argument, with key words in a heavier weight. A fading staircase of tiny gold crosses sits in empty corners. The cover is pure golden light with soft, broad sun beams. Signature: a halftone sunburst of gold dots and crosses rising out of black.

## Signature moves
1. Sand-paper grounds for content; near-black chapter and quote pages; a golden sun-light cover.
2. Small regular-weight titles pinned top-left; nothing shouts.
3. Narrow reading columns on the right; generous empty space everywhere else.
4. A golden halftone sunburst (dots and crosses) breaking in from the right edge of chapter pages.
5. Key words in a heavier weight inside an otherwise regular paragraph; a faint sun mark on the sign-off.

## Never
- Large or heavy headlines
- Bright accent colours on content pages (gold only, used as light)
- Boxes, cards with borders, or drop shadows
- Centred layouts on content pages
- More than one pattern on a slide

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** brand identity books, energy and climate start-ups, calm investor overviews, product and strategy narratives
- **Not for:** loud campaign pitches, playful youth brands, data-dense dashboards
