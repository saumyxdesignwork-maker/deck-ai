# Editorial — Style DNA

Warm paper pages with ink-black interludes and one signal-red chapter panel. A Swiss grotesk works at extreme contrast: giant tight words and condensed bold caps against tiny mono meta in the corners. A single italic serif word slips into headlines, and one word per slide may be ringed in hand-drawn red. Black-and-white photography is structural: full-bleed covers, half panels, strips and grids, never decoration. Notes are numbered "01/" and divided by hairlines, with a vertical marker running down the right edge. Signature: one enormous word, one red mark, everything else whispered.

## Signature moves
1. Giant single words or condensed caps headlines at poster scale.
2. Black-and-white photography as layout blocks (half panels, strips, grids).
3. One red: a full chapter panel, a giant figure, or a hand-drawn ring around one word.
4. An italic serif word inside grotesk headlines.
5. Tiny mono meta: header row, "01/" notes, vertical edge marker.

## Never
- More than one red element per slide (except a red chapter panel)
- Colour photography (it goes grayscale unless a brand palette says otherwise)
- Gradients, glows, shadows or rounded cards
- Centred body text (only short statements may centre)
- More than two typefaces per slide besides the mono meta

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** brand and creative proposals, fashion, architecture, studio credentials, campaign decks
- **Not for:** dense data reporting, technical documentation, decks without any photography
