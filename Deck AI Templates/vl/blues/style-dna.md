# Blues — Style DNA

Midnight navy grounds lit by electric cobalt and violet light, through vertical streaks, horizon glows and diagonal beams, with periwinkle chapter slides and an ice-white closing. A light, tight grotesk sets every headline in sentence case, softly graded from white to lavender, with the lead-in words muted. Small mono labels and "(01)" tags mark structure, and quiet footer chrome runs along the bottom. Content sits on frosted glass cards with hairline borders, and the one thing that matters flips to a solid white card. Signature: giant ghost numerals that fade into the ground on chapter slides.

## Signature moves
1. Light as material: vertical streaks, horizon glows and diagonal beams in cobalt to violet.
2. Frosted glass cards, with exactly one inverted white card for the hero item.
3. Giant ghost numerals on periwinkle chapter slides, bleeding off the edge.
4. Light grotesk headlines with a soft gradient fill and a muted lead-in.
5. Mono "(01)" tags and bottom footer chrome.

## Never
- Warm colours (no red, orange or yellow except in a chart series)
- Heavy or bold display weights
- More than one inverted white card per slide
- Hard drop shadows (glow only)
- Busy photography (any photo sits behind a blue duotone)

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** SaaS and fintech product decks, investor updates, tech launches, platform overviews
- **Not for:** warm consumer brands, hospitality, food, anything that needs earthy or human warmth
