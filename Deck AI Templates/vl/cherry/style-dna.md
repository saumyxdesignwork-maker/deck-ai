# Cherry — Style DNA

Warm black and blush cream trade places slide by slide, sometimes on the same slide as split halves, with one cherry-red ground saved for chapter breaks. A condensed Didone in caps shouts at poster scale, bleeding to the frame and tagged with a small red index, while a quiet neo-grotesk does all the explaining. Labels are tiny tracked caps, and the section tag sits in a hairline box top-left. Cherry red is the only colour: every number, bar, dot, pill and highlight is red. Photographs are drenched in red light. Signature: a glowing red-to-magenta ring around a black disk opens and closes the deck.

## Signature moves
1. Giant Didone caps words with a red parenthetical index "(01)".
2. One hue only: cherry red for all data and emphasis, on black or blush.
3. Black and blush rhythm, including split half-and-half slides.
4. Boxed section tag top-left and red duration pills.
5. A glowing ring on the cover and the closing slide.

## Never
- A second accent colour (magenta lives only inside the ring glow)
- Serif for body text or small labels
- Sentence-case serif headlines (the serif is always caps)
- Rounded corners above 8px
- Unfiltered colour photography
- More than two slides in a row on the same ground

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** proposals, pitches, agency credentials, strategy decks, investor narratives
- **Not for:** academic or medical content, anything that needs a calm or neutral tone
