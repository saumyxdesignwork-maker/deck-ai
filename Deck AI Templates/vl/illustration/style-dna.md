# Illustration — Style DNA

Warm cream pages interleaved with flat chapter colours: sky, burnt orange, peach, mustard. Heavy, tight grotesk caps are set huge, sometimes broken across lines, while a condensed serif does the reading and turns statistics into big centred sentences. Thin black hairlines split slides into columns, and black rounded tiles hold the numbers. Every chapter has a character: flat, bold-outlined people in polka dots and stripes, in full colour on light pages and black line art on colour. Signature: giant caps tangled up with a cast of illustrated people.

## Signature moves
1. Original flat character illustrations with bold outlines and dot or stripe clothing.
2. Heavy tight grotesk caps at poster scale.
3. Condensed serif for reading, and large centred serif for statistic statements.
4. Flat colour chapter grounds that cycle (sky, orange, peach, mustard).
5. Thin vertical hairlines and black rounded number tiles.

## Never
- Gradients, glows or shadows
- Photography in place of illustration (photos are allowed only in a supplied image slot)
- Thin or light display weights
- More than one illustration style in a deck
- Colour illustrations on saturated grounds (switch to line art)

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** reports, research findings, education and training, culture and people topics, onboarding
- **Not for:** luxury or premium brand pitches, dense financial data, anything that needs a serious, sober tone
