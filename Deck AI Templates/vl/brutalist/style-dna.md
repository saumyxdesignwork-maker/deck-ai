# Brutalist — Style DNA

Loud flat colour blocks colliding on every slide: acid lime, bubblegum pink, coral red, kelly green and marigold, with black for punctuation. Titles are heavy retro display capitals; subheads use the same face, small; reading text is a plain grotesque in black. Every slide stacks two or three blocks of clashing colour: a panel bleeding off an edge, a quarter-circle in a corner, a square tucked under a photo. Starbursts, four-point sparkles and zigzag strips sit on top. Photos are saturated and set into the blocks like cut-outs. Signature: a starburst on the shoulder of a giant headline.

## Signature moves
1. A different flat ground on every slide, cycling through five acid colours, with black as a rare reset.
2. Two or three clashing blocks per slide, never the ground's own colour, always flat with hard edges or perfect arcs.
3. Heavy retro display caps for titles and subheads; plain black grotesque for reading text.
4. Starbursts, sparkles and zigzag strips as punctuation, one or two per slide.
5. Saturated photos set into colour blocks; cards, tiers and data drawn as solid colour shapes.

## Never
- Gradients, shadows, glass or transparency
- Rounded-corner cards (only full circles and quarter-circles)
- Grey or muted grounds
- Lowercase or light-weight headlines
- More than one starburst competing on a slide

## Best for / not for
- **Best for:** brand portfolios, agency and studio credentials, campaign pitches, youth, music, food and fashion brands
- **Not for:** finance, legal or medical decks; dense data reporting; sombre subjects
