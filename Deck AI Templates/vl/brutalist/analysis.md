# Brutalist — reference analysis

One 18-slide brand-portfolio board supplied by the user (20 Oct 2026). Source and licence are not recorded, so we extract principles only. No text, names, people or photographs are reproduced.

| Dimension | Observed | Interpretation (what we build) |
|---|---|---|
| Grounds | A different saturated flat colour on nearly every slide (lime, pink, red, green, marigold); one or two black slides | Five `ground.accent` colours, ground rotation `cycle`; quote slides on black |
| Colour blocks | Every slide layers 2–3 more flat blocks: panels bleeding off an edge, quarter- and half-circles, squares under photos | Per-ground clash colours `--b1..3` in the skin; frame ornaments (quarter disc, corner stack, zigzag strip, wave band) on shared layouts; blocks built into the Brutalist layouts |
| Display type | Heavy retro geometric capitals, very bold, slightly quirky ("BRAND PORTFOLIO", "BREAK SLIDE") | Chango (OFL), uppercase, for titles, subheads and labels |
| Reading type | Small plain sans paragraphs, often in two columns | Archivo, black; two-column body on the split layout |
| Marks | Starbursts (solid spiky, and thin-ray), four-point sparkles, zigzag lines in a strip, wavy lines in a band | SVG ornaments: `burst`, `burst-rays`, `sparkles`, `ray-star`, `zig-box`, `fr-strip`, `fr-band` |
| Imagery | Saturated fashion/youth photos set into colour panels, often cut by a block or circle | Photo slots inside blocks; a mild saturation lift; app fill = colour block with a starburst |
| Composition | Collage: text on one side, a block-and-photo stack on the other; giant headline break slides; a 2×2 checker sign-off | New layouts `title/block-collage`, `text/block-split`, `section/burst`, `closing/block-checker` |
| Signature | "A brand zine made of coloured paper and a starburst stamp" | See style-dna.md |
