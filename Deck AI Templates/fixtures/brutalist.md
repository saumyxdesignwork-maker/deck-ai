### Story of the brand
# Born on a *market stall*
We started by painting signs for a Saturday food market. Traders kept asking for menus, then logos, then whole shopfronts.

Five years later the market is still a client, and the same rule applies to every brief: if it would not stop someone walking past, it is not finished.
