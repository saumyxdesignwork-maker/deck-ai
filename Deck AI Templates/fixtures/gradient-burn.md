### 2027 Outlook
# Afterglow
The creator economy after the hype

---

# 01 The hype cooled faster than the spend
Attention moved on. Budgets did not.

---

### 01 — The shift
# Audiences are still here
## They stopped arriving by accident.

Discovery feeds now reward what people already follow. Reach from strangers fell for most mid-sized creators, while revenue from existing fans kept climbing.

The winners did not chase the algorithm back. They built places their audience returns to on purpose.

---

### 01 — The shift
# What changed in twelve months
- **−41%** Reach from strangers — Median mid-sized creator
- **+63%** Revenue per returning fan — Memberships and drops {emphasis}
- **3.2×** Owned-channel signups — Email and community

> Source: Panel of 1,200 creators, Jan–Sep 2026

# 02 Building for the return visit

---

### 02 — Playbook
# Six habits of the creators who grew
- **A weekly ritual** — One fixed format people plan around
- **An owned list** — Every post points to a place you control
- **Fewer, bigger drops** — Scarcity beats a constant stream
- **Fans as cast** — Put the audience inside the work
- **Paid depth** — Free reach, paid context
- **One metric** — Returning viewers, not views

---

### 02 — Playbook
# The number to watch
- **38%** Of revenue now comes from fans in their second year — Up from 21% in 2025
