<!-- archetype: bullets; variant: two-column -->
# Our values
- **Slow by design** We make products for the few quiet minutes people give themselves
- **Gentle on skin** Short ingredient lists, nothing that stings or perfumes for show
- **Honest claims** We say what a product does and stop there
- **Kind packaging** Refillable glass and paper, no plastic outers
