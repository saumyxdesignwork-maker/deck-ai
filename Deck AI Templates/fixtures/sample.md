# 01 Where we are
What six weeks of the pilot told us

---

### 01 — Context
# The pilot proved demand, not retention
## Members joined fast. Most did not come back a second time.

Across 41 partner stores, sign-ups beat forecast in the first fortnight. The welcome offer did its job: people scanned, joined and redeemed.

What it did not do was build a habit. Second visits flattened by week four, and the members we did keep were the ones who already shopped weekly.

---

### 02 — Results
# Six weeks, measurable lift
- **12,842** Check-ins — Across 41 partner stores
- **+38%** Repeat visits — vs. same period 2025 {emphasis}
- **500** New members — From the weekend drop

> Source: Internal CRM, Sep 2026

---

### 02 — Results
# One number matters
- **₹1,24,500 Cr** Gross merchandise value influenced — Upper bound; see appendix for method

---

### 03 — What changes
# Four moves for phase two
- **Weekly challenges** — Small, repeatable reasons to come back every week
- **Partner bundles** — Pair two stores so one visit earns at both
- **Tiered rewards** — Status that members can see and lose
- **Local drops** — Limited items announced only inside the app

---

### 03 — What changes
# What we heard from store managers
- Sign-up at the till slows the queue at peak hours
- Staff want a single screen that shows member status
- The printed card is still requested by older customers
- Redemption codes are hard to read aloud
- Weekend staffing does not match drop timing
- Managers want weekly, not monthly, numbers
- Two stores asked for their own local offers
