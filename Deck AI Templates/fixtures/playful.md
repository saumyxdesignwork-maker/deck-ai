# project *name*
## Wall Stories: a summer residency where young artists paint the story of their own street.
Each neighbourhood gets one wall, one lead artist and a crew of eight young painters aged 14 to 19. They spend two weeks listening, sketching and voting on a design before anything goes on the wall.

The finished murals form a walking route across the city, with a printed map and an audio guide recorded by the crews themselves.

Everything is documented, so the method can travel to other cities next year.

# project stages
Two weeks of listening before a single line is painted.
- **Stage 1** Listen and sketch — Street interviews, photo walks and open sketch sessions with residents of all ages
- **Stage 2** Design and paint — Crews vote on a final design, then paint over ten days with a lead artist on site

---

<!-- archetype: timeline; variant: vertical-title -->
# timeline
Seven weeks from first workshop to opening party. Each wall runs on the same rhythm, staggered by one week across the city.
- **Week 1** Kick-off — Meet the crews, walk the street
- **Week 2** Listen — Interviews and photo walks
- **Week 3** Sketch — Open sketch sessions
- **Week 4** Vote — Residents choose the design
- **Week 5** Prep — Wall cleaned, primed and gridded
- **Week 6** Paint — Ten days on the wall
- **Week 7** Open — Block party and walking map

# outcomes
- **Six new murals** — One per neighbourhood, each designed by the people who live there
- **48 young artists** — Paid, trained and credited on every wall
- **A walking route** — A printed map and audio guide linking all six walls
- **Open method** — A free toolkit so other cities can run their own
- **Local partners** — Shops and schools sponsoring paint and space
- **A documentary** — Short film following one crew from sketch to opening
