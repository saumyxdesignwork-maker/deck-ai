# About *Aster Power*
Aster Power is a renewable electricity trading start-up based in Osaka. It uses AI-driven forecasting to match solar and wind producers with buyers every fifteen minutes, so clean power is used where it is generated instead of being curtailed.

The company sits where energy markets meet software: technical enough for traders, simple enough for a facilities manager buying power for a single building.

---

<!-- archetype: text; variant: statement-plain -->
# The new identity focuses on ==clarity, precision and market relevance==. It balances AI-driven sophistication with warmth, using quiet type and structured layouts to build trust, while a golden sun motif keeps a clear link to ==renewable energy==.
Understated Japanese print references and vintage warmth stop the system from feeling cold.
