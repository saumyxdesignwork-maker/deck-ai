# A note to you
## Built for clarity and measurable progress.

Halden has the product and the shelf space. What it lacks is a single plan that marketing, sales and supply all work from.

This proposal sets out that plan: where growth will come from, what we will build in the first sixteen weeks, and how we will know it is working.
