### Problem
# Why freight goes dark between handoffs
Most mid-size carriers still stitch together calls, spreadsheets and driver texts. Every handoff is a place where a shipment disappears from view.
- **Status lives in five different tools** — Dispatch, telematics, email, spreadsheets and the driver's phone never agree
- **Delays surface after the customer notices** — Exceptions are found by phone calls, hours after they start {emphasis}
- **Proof of delivery arrives days late** — Paper slips slow invoicing and dispute every second claim
