## Prompt for ChatGPT

> A cinematic photograph of a single person walking away down a long warehouse aisle, seen from behind, lit only by deep red light from above. Tall shelving on both sides, slight haze, soft shadows, shallow depth of field. Portrait orientation 3:4. Plenty of dark empty space in the lower third. No text, no logos, no visible faces.

## Fit requirements (I will check these)
- **Ratio:** portrait, about 3:4 (the card is ~600×800 at 1920 width)
- **Focal point:** the figure sits in the upper-middle of the frame; the lower third stays dark, because the caption sits there
- **Treatment:** any colour works. Cherry converts it to grayscale and multiplies it with cherry red, so tonal contrast matters more than hue
- **Avoid:** faces, logos, text, bright skies (they turn flat pink under the red multiply)
